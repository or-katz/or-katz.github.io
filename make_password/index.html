How the words are made

No word or syllable lists are used. Each word is built from English sounds: a vowel framed by consonant clusters that English sound rules allow (like str-, bl-, -nt, -sk), then spelled with English spelling rules (silent e, ck / tch / dge, doubled letters). Words are combined into short sentences with grammar endings (-s, -ed, -y, -ly), so they are easy to say. Randomness comes from your browser's secure random generator, and nothing leaves this page.
